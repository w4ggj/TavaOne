Will RADE Be the Next SSB?

ARRL News

If you're into digital modes at all, this one's worth your time — ARRL is asking whether RADE, the new codec-based voice mode, could eventually do to SSB what SSB did to AM back in the day. With its spectrum efficiency and modern design, it's shaping up to be a serious contender, and it'll be interesting to see how the HF community warms up to it.

FCC and HHS Initiate New Proceedings on RF Exposure

ARRL News

Heads up, gang — both the FCC and the Department of Health and Human Services have opened new proceedings on RF exposure, and ARRL is keeping a close eye on how things develop. This is one to watch carefully, since whatever comes out of these proceedings could have real implications for how we operate, especially those of us running higher power on HF.
