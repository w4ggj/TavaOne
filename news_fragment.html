Will RADE Be the Next SSB?

ARRL News

If you're into digital modes like FT8, you'll want to keep an eye on RADE — it could be the next big shift in HF voice operating, just like SSB was fifty years ago. ARRL is asking whether this new codec-based mode has what it takes to finally modernize how hams talk on the bands.

FCC and HHS Initiate New Proceedings on RF Exposure

ARRL News

This one's worth watching closely — the FCC and the Department of Health and Human Services both kicked off new proceedings on RF exposure back on September 17th, and ARRL is keeping tabs on how it could affect us as hams. Whether you're running QRP or pushing a kilowatt, any changes to exposure rules could have real impact on how we operate.
