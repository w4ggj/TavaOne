Former ARRL COO Harold R. Kramer, WJ1B, Silent Key

ARRL News

Sad news from Newington — Harold Kramer, WJ1B, who served as ARRL Chief Operating Officer and QST Publisher from 2005 until his retirement, became a Silent Key on August 31 at age 78. He was a major figure in shaping the League during a pivotal era for amateur radio, and the community is poorer for his passing. 73, Harold.
