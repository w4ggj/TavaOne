Contest University Celebrates 100 Years of Yagis

ARRL News

Hard to believe, but the Yagi-Uda antenna turns 100 years old this year — invented all the way back in 1926 — and Contest University threw a live Zoom webinar to celebrate the centennial this past Saturday. If you missed it, keep an eye out for a recording, because the history of that antenna is absolutely foundational to everything we do on HF.
