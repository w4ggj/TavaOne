DXer and DXpeditioner Kan Mizoguchi, JA1BK, Silent Key

ARRL News

The DX community lost a true legend — Kiyoshi "Kan" Mizoguchi, JA1BK, CQ DX Hall of Famer, prolific DXpeditioner, and former IARU Region 3 Director, passed away in May 2026. His contributions to DXing and the global amateur radio community were enormous, and he'll be greatly missed by hams around the world.
