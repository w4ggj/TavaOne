The ARRL Solar Update: M3.6 Flare Highlights Moderate Activity Period

ARRL News

Region 4493 punched out an M3.6 flare on July 22 — the biggest event of the period — along with a 200 sfu radio burst, so conditions have been interesting if not spectacular lately. Keep an eye on the solar flux if you're chasing DX or planning your next FT8 session, as moderate activity can still produce some nice openings on the higher bands.

DXer and DXpeditioner Kan Mizoguchi, JA1BK, Silent Key

ARRL News

The DX community lost a true legend this past May with the passing of Kiyoshi "Kan" Mizoguchi, JA1BK — a CQ DX Hall of Famer, prolific DXpeditioner, and former IARU Region 3 Director who dedicated decades to putting rare entities on the air for chasers worldwide. If you've ever logged a rare one from a JA-organized DXpedition, there's a good chance Kan had a hand in making it happen — 73, old man, well deserved rest.
