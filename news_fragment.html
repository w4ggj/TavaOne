FCC Cites GMRS Licensee for Illegal Linked Repeater Network

ARRL News

The FCC Enforcement Bureau issued a Notice of Violation to a Georgia man for running a network of nearly two dozen illegally linked GMRS repeaters — a good reminder that the Commission is still paying attention to what's happening on the airwaves. Worth a read for anyone curious about where the lines are drawn between GMRS and amateur radio linking rules.

Colombian Emergency Network Activated Following Earthquake

ARRL News

Amateur radio operators in Colombia activated their emergency communications network following a significant earthquake, with IARU Region 2 Emergency Coordinator Carlos Alberto Santamaría González CO2JC leading the coordinated response. It's a powerful reminder of why we train for emcomm — when the infrastructure goes down, hams step up.
