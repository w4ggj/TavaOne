FCC Proposes New Commercial Satellite Allocations Overlapping Amateur Spectrum

RSGB RadCom / GB2RS

Heads up — the FCC is floating yet another proposal to let commercial satellites move into our spectrum, this time eyeing the 900 MHz, 2.4 GHz, and 5.7 GHz bands, which could spell trouble for Part 97 operations. The ARRL is already engaged on this one, so stay tuned and make sure your voice gets heard through the comment process.
