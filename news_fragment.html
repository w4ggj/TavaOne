ARRL News

The DX community lost a true giant with the passing of Kan Mizoguchi, JA1BK, a CQ DX Hall of Famer and former IARU Region 3 Director who dedicated his life to amateur radio and DXpeditions. A heartfelt 73 to his family and friends — his contributions to the hobby will be felt for a long time to come.
