The ARRL Solar Update: M3.6 Flare Highlights Active Period

ARRL News

Solar activity bumped up to moderate levels this past week with Region 4493 throwing out an M3.6 flare on July 22 — the biggest event of the period — along with a 200 solar flux unit radio burst on 10.7 cm. If you've been chasing DX on 10 or 12 meters, keep an eye on propagation because things could get interesting in either direction depending on what the sun decides to do next.

DXer and DXpeditioner Kan Mizoguchi, JA1BK, Silent Key

ARRL News

The DX community lost a true legend — Kiyoshi "Kan" Mizoguchi, JA1BK, CQ DX Hall of Famer, prolific DXpeditioner, and former IARU Region 3 Director, passed away in May 2026. If you've ever worked a rare one from a remote Pacific or Asian entity over the decades, there's a real chance Kan had a hand in making it happen — 73, old man, well deserved rest.
