Will RADE Be the Next SSB?

ARRL News

Could RADE do for voice what FT8 did for weak-signal digital — completely reshape how we operate? ARRL is making the case that this new codec-based voice mode has the efficiency and performance chops to dethrone SSB, just like SSB pushed AM off the throne 50 years ago. Worth a read if you care about the future of HF voice.
