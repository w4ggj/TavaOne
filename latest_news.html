Former ARRL COO Harold R. Kramer, WJ1B, Silent Key

ARRL News

Sad news from the ARRL — Harold Kramer, WJ1B, who served as Chief Operating Officer and QST Publisher from 2005 onward, became a Silent Key on August 31st at age 78. He was a dedicated servant to this hobby and the League for many years, and the amateur radio community is lesser for his passing. 73, Harold.
