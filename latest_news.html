FCC Proposes New Commercial Satellite Allocations Overlapping Amateur Spectrum

Heads up — the FCC is floating another proposal that would let commercial satellites operate in the 900 MHz, 2.4 GHz, and 5.7 GHz bands, all of which overlap existing amateur allocations. The ARRL is keeping an eye on this one, and so should we.
